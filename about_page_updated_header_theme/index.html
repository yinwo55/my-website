Inwoo Lee

Geopolitical & AI Policy Analyst

The contest over AI is not primarily a technology competition. AI is a general-purpose technology of the kind that historically reshapes the international system: driving creative destruction, realigning winners and losers, and triggering the power transitions that will define the next century of global governance. That insight drives my work, producing decision-relevant intelligence on U.S.-China strategic competition, where the outcome of the AI rivalry will set the rules of the international order for generations.

I specialize in making that rivalry legible: translating Mandarin-language primary sources (policy documents, financial disclosures, technical publications) into decision-relevant intelligence for government and institutional stakeholders, covering foreign policy, industrial strategy, AI rivalry, and semiconductor competition. Over four-plus years at Korean policy research institutes, I delivered analytical products across 30 commissioned projects to senior officials at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Ministry of Unification, Korea Foundation, and other government institutions. Beyond primary research, I edited dual China policy publications, Sungkyun China Brief and Sungkyun China Insight, and coordinated Track 1.5 and II dialogues engaging government officials and researchers from Korea, China, and Taiwan. Doctoral scholarship at Indiana University subsequently added the computational foundation, including applied statistics, NLP, and topic modeling, that empirical research on AI competition demands.
